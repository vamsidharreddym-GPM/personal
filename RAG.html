Chunking

Documents are divided into smaller passages because retrieval works better when the system searches focused pieces of information instead of massive documents.

Embeddings

An embedding converts text into numbers representing aspects of its meaning. Semantically related text tends to occupy nearby regions of vector space.

Retrieval

The system searches for document vectors most similar to the user's query vector and retrieves the associated original text.

Generation

Retrieved passages are inserted into the LLM's context. The model generates a response using both its instructions and the newly retrieved information.
